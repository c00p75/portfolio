## What it is

A seven-page site (home, about, team, warehousing, blog, posts, contact) for a
commodity trading company. The pages that matter commercially are warehousing and
the blog: one says what Seneca can physically handle, the other shows that it
follows the markets it trades in.

## The blog

Posts live in a single typed data module rather than a CMS, which suits a small
team that publishes occasionally. The rendering side does the work: headings
become anchors, the contents list builds itself, and every post goes out with
structured data so search engines read it as an article.
