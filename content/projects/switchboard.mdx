## What it is

Project management built around AI: an agent that understands bulk edits on the
board, extraction of epics and stories from product documents as they stream in,
and scenario analysis that compares the current trajectory against changes in
team size, sprint length and scope. Stories also move automatically when their
linked pull requests change state, and agents can read the board through an MCP
endpoint.
