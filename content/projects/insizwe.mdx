## What it is

A broker sells trust and a quick answer to "how much will this cost". The site
leads with both: a plain-language home page, and a quote wizard that gives a real
indicative number in four steps instead of a "we'll call you" form.

## From quote to follow-up

A quote isn't the end of the journey; it's the start of a sales conversation. So
every submission becomes a lead with a status (new, contacted, qualified, won,
lost or spam), a reference number, notes that keep their author even after a
staff account is removed, and the marketing source that brought the visitor in.

## What isn't built yet

There is a customer portal page, but it's a placeholder; the work so far is the
public site, the pricing and the staff side.
