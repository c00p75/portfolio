## What it is

A six-page site (home, about, products, services, markets, contact) for a company
whose product is literally a pole in the ground. Rather than stock photos of
timber, the site draws its subject: power lines, a pole yard at night, a pole on
its way from treatment to a house.

## The flock

The wordmark is not an image. The text is drawn once to a hidden canvas, every
opaque pixel above a threshold becomes a target, and ten thousand birds fly to
them. The logo's "A" mark is sampled the same way after cropping it by scanning
for empty rows and columns.

## What isn't wired yet

The quotation form is laid out and labelled properly but doesn't send anything
yet; it asks visitors to call the sales desk.
