## What it is

Five pages (home, about, services, events, contact) for a professional services
firm whose revenue comes from training seats and advisory work. The site leads
with courses and events rather than a generic "about us".

## Registration without a backend

The firm needed bookings, not accounts. Choosing an event opens a form pre-filled
with that event's name and fee; the submission is emailed straight to the firm,
with clear success and failure states that close themselves after a few seconds.
